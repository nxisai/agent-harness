# Changelog

## 0.16.1 (2026-11-28)

A fix for full screen on top of 0.16.0.

### Fixed

- **Step cards keep their shape in full screen.** An agent's step card (a request or booking review, a form) no longer spans the whole conversation: it is capped at 560px, so its primary button isn't stretched across the screen and its labels stay next to their values. The window view is unchanged.

## 0.16.0 (2026-11-24)

Quotes and bookings drawn as step cards in the chat, replies in markdown with citations, and the visitor's details asked once per conversation. Many fixes to hours, citations, handoffs and step cards.

### Added

- **Step surfaces: quotes and bookings in the chat.** A handoff target can draw its steps under its reply instead of asking for everything in words: a guided intake, an estimate with its sources, the review the visitor sends, a day and arrival-window picker with the hold's countdown, the confirmation, and a receipt with Add to calendar and Reschedule. See docs/quote-booking-surfaces.md.
  - **On the wire.** The target sends A2UI v1.0 messages (`createSurface`, `updateComponents`, `updateDataModel`, `deleteSurface`) in a `surface` part of the handoff extension, beside its text. They are drawn with the steps catalog, `https://nxis.ai/a2ui/catalogs/steps/v1.json`: the basic catalog's Card, Column, Row, List, Text, Button, ChoicePicker, TextField, CheckBox, DateTimeInput, Divider and Icon, plus `DayStrip`, `WindowGrid`, `HoldCountdown`, `DateTile`, `EstimateCard`, `ConfirmCard` and `ReceiptCard`. A choice or a press comes back on the same task as the visitor's words and an `action` part (`surfaceId`, `name`, `component`, the resolved `context`). The envelope's `ui` gains `surfaces: 1`.
  - **Only the visitor commits.** `commit: true`, with `consent: { rows, at }`, is sent only for a `ConfirmCard`'s confirm button, which shows exactly what will be sent; the page reads it from the component, never from what the target wrote. Once an action is out, every surface waits for the answer; a confirmed card stays done until the target sends it again; when the handoff is over, all are inert.
  - **Read strictly.** Only the four message kinds and the components above; no Image, Video, AudioPlayer, Tabs, Modal or Slider; event actions only, no `functionCall` and no `openUrl`; no prototype keys; at most 20 messages and 48 KB a part, 150 components a surface and 8 surfaces a conversation. What breaks a rule is dropped. `nxis.back` (Back to the assistant) and `nxis.open` (a `tel:` number, or an https link on this page's origin, a `link-origins` origin or the relay's, after the guardrails' navigation check) are the page's own and never sent.
  - **Accessible and themed.** Every choice is a real button of at least 44 px with `aria-pressed`, a full day or window is disabled and says Full, the countdown is a timer that announces only its end, the date tile reads the whole date, and the estimate's source markers are the Support answer's, moving to their row in the Sources list. Colors are the element's tokens, so a dark theme applies; icons are Heroicons.
  - **Kept.** The conversation keeps each surface as last updated, so a reload draws it the same. `HandoffController.send` takes extension parts beside the words. `./a2ui` exports `readSurfaceMessages`, `STEPS_CATALOG`, `STEP_COMPONENTS`, `BASIC_ALLOWED`, `PAGE_EVENTS`, `SURFACE_LIMITS`, `surfaceIdOf`, `foldSurface` and the `SurfaceAction`, `SurfaceEntry` and `ConfirmRow` types.
  - **Sizes.** The components, the strict reading and the routing are a chunk of their own, about 8.3 KB gzip under a new `surfaces` budget of 9,216 B, loaded with the first reply that has a surface. The ui page-load budget's margin rises from 18,588 to 19,148 B (+560 B) for reading the part, sending the action, the thread item, loading the chunk and the renderer kit handed to it. `zod` (already bundled through the A2UI renderer) is now a direct dependency; `jsdom` is a dev dependency, for the surfaces' tests.

- **Field markers and errors on step surfaces (`ui.surfaces` 3).** A TextField and a ChoiceChips take `required` and `optional`, drawn as "Required" or "Optional" after the label, and `error`, a dynamic string drawn as a warn FieldNote under that field (the field `aria-invalid`, naming it in `aria-describedby`). Bind each field's error to a path of its own and fill them in one `updateDataModel`, so every problem shows at once, in place. The steps catalog's TextField is now its own, with the basic one's props. The envelope's `ui` is `{ cards: 2, feedback: 1, surfaces: 3 }`. See docs/quote-booking-surfaces.md.
  - **Sizes.** The `surfaces` budget rises from 10,752 to 11,264 B (10,873 B with it).
- **Replies drawn as markdown.** The page assistant's bubbles, a remote agent's replies and A2UI Text draw a safe subset: paragraphs, bold and italic, bulleted and numbered lists, inline code, headings and links. No raw HTML: only the harness's own elements are built, every string set as text. A link is drawn only when the page allows its origin, and opens only past the guardrails' navigation check. The element is the markdown renderer A2UI Text asks its Lit context for, so the renderer's "no markdown renderer is configured" warning is gone.
- **Citations on the page assistant's answers.** An answer from records read in the turn (`nxis_query`, `nxis_get`) gets the numbered markers and Sources list a remote answer has, drawn by the same view: a line or sentence that names a record (its name, phone number or street address) cites it. The model is asked nothing new. The records are kept with the message, so a stored conversation draws them again.
  - **Sizes.** The renderer is a lazy `markdown` chunk (about 1.7 KB gzip); matching an answer to its records is in the lazy `sources` chunk (about 3.7 KB). The ui page-load budget's margin rises by 837 B for the loader, the bubble fill, the context provider and the link check.
- **The visitor gives their details once per conversation.** What they type or confirm with one agent (name, email, phone, street, city, ZIP code: a form they send, a target's step they continue, a target's hand-back `contact`) is kept with the conversation, over the host's preferences, and sent to every later agent target as the envelope's `contact`, so Quote then Booking asks once. A reload keeps it; "New conversation" and deleting the conversation drop it; it is never written to `preferences` or their storage, never sent to a person desk, and the model reads it only inside the saved profile's data block. A target's `clear_details` event ("Not you? Clear details") clears it. `PROFILE_ALIASES` gains `city` and `zip`; the handoff module exports `readContact`, `CONTACT_FIELDS` and the `HandoffContact` type, and `HandBack` has `contact`. See docs/handoff.md, "The visitor's details".
  - **Sizes.** The ui page-load budget's margin rises from 21,275 to 21,923 B (+648 B).
- **What a step surface's TextField holds.** `inputType` (`email`, `tel`, `url`, `numeric`) and `autocomplete`, for the visitor's keyboard and autofill: an email input, a phone keypad, a numeric keypad for a ZIP code.
- **`inset` on EstimateCard, ConfirmCard and ReceiptCard**, for one inside a Card under its StepHeader.
- **A field's error clears as the visitor edits it.** Typing in a TextField or picking a chip clears that field's bound `error` on the page, until the target checks it again.

### Changed

An accent alone now brings its pressed shade and its wash, so a chosen chip, the markers and the handoff bar read in the site's color. - **The handoff bar is at the top**, under the header, in the accent's wash: "**Get a quote** · handed off from the assistant", and Back to assistant. A person is still "Talking with …". It wraps rather than cutting its words short, and stays a `status`.
- **An estimate's Sources go under its card**, after the estimate, rather than in the reply's bubble.
- **No schema.org names for visitors.** A record card's type tag is its type in words, and a search's plumbing records are left out beside the things they came with. What the model reads is unchanged.
  - **Sizes.** The ui page-load budget's margin rises by 974 B.
- **Skipped site actions are logged once, at debug level.** An action whose form is on another host, or that has no page, is expected in a site's records; the element skips it. That was a warning on every page load. It is now said once a tab session with `console.debug`, naming the other origins and how many have no page. +124 B on the ui page-load budget's margin, which rises from 19,340 to 21,275 B in all.

## 0.15.3

Two fixes for pages in Chrome with native WebMCP. Every turn there failed in 0.15.0 to 0.15.2.

### Fixed

- **Site tools keep their names in Chrome's native WebMCP.** In Chrome, `document.modelContext.getTools()` returns `Tool` objects whose fields are accessors on the prototype, and the guardrails copied each tool with an object spread to wrap its description, which copies none of them. Every tool but the harness's own reached the model with a description and no name or schema, and a model proxy's API refused the whole request (`tools.0.custom.name: Field required`), so every turn failed. Tools are now copied field by field (`name`, `description`, `inputSchema` and its aliases, `annotations`, `origin`), the handoff tools are built rather than spread, and the proxy provider leaves out a tool with no name, with a warning, instead of sending a request that would be refused.
  - **Sizes.** The guardrails chunk's budget rises from 8,560 to 8,640 B (+80 B) and the ui page-load budget's margin from 18,588 to 18,652 B (+64 B).
- **Tools run on Chrome 158.** Chrome 158's native `executeTool(tool, input?, options?)` accepts only the very `Tool` object `getTools()` returned: a copy of it, an object made from it or a name is refused with "parameter 1 is not of type 'Tool'", and a JSON string input with "parameter 2 is not of type 'object'". A tool the harness holds as a copy (the handoff tools, rebuilt each turn for the targets listed now) or not at all failed every form it tried. When the registry refuses the tool it was given, `executeRegistered` now reads the registry's own object for that name from `getTools()` and calls it with the same two forms, then falls back to the name as before. Every step is still taken only when the one before was refused for its shape, before anything ran: a tool that ran and failed is never tried again.
  - **Sizes.** The ui page-load budget's margin rises from 18,652 to 18,780 B (+128 B).

## 0.15.2

No change to the package's code. This release is about where it is published from.

### Changed

- **Published to npm from this repository, with provenance.** Each version is now published by this repository's Publish workflow (`.github/workflows/publish.yml`), using npm trusted publishing with `--provenance`, after a maintainer approves it in the `npm` environment. On npm, the version links to the run that built it, and its `repository.url`, `homepage` and `bugs` name `github.com/nxisai/agent-harness`. Earlier versions named a repository that isn't public. Before publishing, the workflow checks that the tag matches `package.json`, that `repository.url` names this repository, that nothing internal is in the tree or in `dist/`, and that typecheck, build and tests pass on Node 20 and 22. CONTRIBUTING.md, "Releases", covers the whole flow and how to verify a version.
- **Checked for anything internal before it ships.** `scripts/check-public.mjs` fails on references to private repositories, issue or pull request references, internal or tunnel hosts, connection strings, non-example email addresses, and a list of internal names, kept as hashes so the file doesn't publish them. It runs on the tree this repository receives, on the npm build (`dist/`) and on the CDN build (`dist-cdn/`). Usage: `node scripts/check-public.mjs <file|dir|.tar.gz>...`.
- **Placeholders in the bench and the tests.** `bench/proxy/.env.example` uses `your-tunnel.example` for the tunnel host. Test fixtures use reserved example domains for email addresses and a business's site.

## 0.15.1

### Fixed

- **A turn no longer ends on result cards with nothing said.** Asked "What are your hours?", the assistant listed the site's record types, drew them as twelve cards (BreadcrumbList 16, Plumber 16, …) and stopped: the harness ran one tool a turn and ended the turn on its results, so the model never saw what came back, never searched, and never answered. A search that did run ended the same way, with location cards and no sentence; the second request a page made in that turn was the recommendation, whose words are not shown.
  - **A read is a step.** Once a read's results are drawn, the model is asked again with them: it calls the next read it needs (`nxis_list_types` to pick a type, then `nxis_query`) or answers in a sentence or two from the results, under the cards. The cards stay.
  - **A type listing is never the answer.** Its result reaches the model as "the kinds of records this site has. To answer, search them with nxis_query next, unless the visitor asked what kinds there are", and `nxis_list_types`'s description says it is a step before `nxis_query`.
  - **Bounded.** At most three reads a turn (`MAX_STEPS`, or the guardrails' `tools.maxPerTurn` when lower); after the last, the model is offered no tools and can only answer. The same read isn't run twice. A reply with neither words nor a call is asked for once more, with no tools and an instruction to answer that is added to the read's result for that request only and never kept; then the cards stand with "Here's what I found." (or "I couldn't find that here." after no records, or after only a type listing). Each is one more completion within the guardrails' step and token budgets. A stop during the answer keeps the cards and drops the unfinished answer.
  - **The answer has the facts.** The model reads back up to 4,000 characters of a result (`RESULT_CHARS`) instead of 400, enough for a page of records' hours.
  - **Still a step to a model proxy.** Every request after the first in a turn ends on the read's result, in its data block, so a proxy counts it as a step of the turn, not as a new message from the visitor. See docs/remote-providers.md.
  - A read the visitor confirmed, or one that waited on a field, is answered the same way once it runs; if the visitor sends another message first, that answer is dropped and the new turn goes on.
  - **Sizes.** The ui page-load budget's margin rises from 17,698 to 18,588 B (+890 B) for the answer step, its bounds, the nudge and the type listing's wording.

## 0.15.0

### Added

- **Handoff targets from A2A agent cards.** With NxisAI's relay, `handoff: { relay }` is enough: the harness reads the site's specialists as agent cards from `<edge>/v1/s/<site>/agents` (beside `…/handoff`), the first time the targets are read and never on page load, keeps them 30 s and reads them again with their ETag. Only a card whose `url` is the relay's own endpoint for the target it names is trusted. The handoff tool's description gives each card's description and skills, and every turn describes the tools from the targets listed now, so a paused specialist is gone from the next turn after a refresh. A page's `targets` work as before, merged with the cards, and a target can name its own card (`card: '/a2a/desk/agent-card.json'`, on the relay's origin) for a self-deployed relay. The card reading is a lazy chunk (1.8 KB gzip); the ui page-load graph is 20 B smaller than before, with no margin change. With `discover="false"`, the handoff tools are now registered at the first open too. See docs/handoff.md.
- **A contained preview: `contained`.** `<nxis-agent contained>` draws the same launcher and window as window mode, with the same markup, CSS, sizes, shadow, header buttons and footer, inside the host element's box instead of fixed to the viewport: 20px from the box's bottom right, at `--window-w` × `--window-h`, capped to the box less 16px a side. The launcher opens it, ✕ closes it back to the launcher (focus returns to the launcher), and `auto-open` starts it open without taking focus from the page. The header's expand button fills the box, with the conversations in a drawer as in a window, and shrinks back; the choice isn't stored. The `mode` attribute, a stored mode and the 520px narrow-viewport rule don't apply. Messages are off: the input and send button are disabled, the placeholder reads "Preview only: messages are off", and the suggestion chips are drawn as a visitor sees them but send nothing; `allow-input` (or `allowInput`) turns them on. It fetches nothing: discovery and site actions are off, `preload` is `none`, and opening loads only a `modelProvider` the host supplied, never the on-device model. Conversations aren't read from or written to storage, and nothing left pending on another page is resumed. Nothing is drawn outside the box. The host gives the element its size. Also a `contained` property. See "Previewing the chat" in the README.
- **Opening and closing from the page: `close()`, `isOpen` and `nxis-agent-toggle`.** `agent.close()` closes the window to the launcher as ✕ does, in every mode, and does nothing when already closed; `agent.isOpen` says whether the window is showing; and the element fires `nxis-agent-toggle` (`detail: { open }`, bubbling and composed) once for each change, from the launcher, ✕, `open()`, `close()` or `auto-open`. ✕ (and `close()`) now move focus that was inside the agent to the launcher in every mode, rather than leaving it on the hidden button; focus elsewhere on the page stays put.
- **Event cards in the chat, as the NxisAI Hub draws them.** Events (`Event`, `Festival`, `ConferenceEvent` and schema.org's other event types) now draw as their own card rather than as generic records: a date tile, the name and its kind, and a pill with the status (Cancelled, Postponed, Rescheduled, Moved online, Past) or the access (Free, a price or "From $48", Sold out, "On sale Jun 1"); then when, in the event's own time zone with the zone named when it differs from the visitor's, where (the venue and city, "Online", or both), the date a rescheduled event moved from, who runs it and who's on (a venue or person the site holds asks for its card); then Get tickets or Register, Add to calendar and Directions; then its days or sessions, three until "Show all". In a list they are rows, grouped under "Events", upcoming soonest first and past ones last. Every value comes from the record: one with no dates, place or offers shows none, and no offers is never "Free".
  - **Get tickets** opens the site's ticket form on its own page (`nxis:targetKind: selfPage`, `nxis:pageUrl`): on that page the form starts in the chat, as a handoff link starts it; on another page of the site the visitor is handed to it (`?nxis_handoff=…&nxis_report=…`) and the form opens in the same conversation there. With no page for the form, the first offer on sale opens its `url`; with neither, there is no button. A video's or an image's row in a list gets its tile; an episode's row stays bare. A picture that fails to load gives way to the tile it stands in for.
- **Records in any JSON-LD spelling.** `prop()` reads `https://schema.org/x`, `http://schema.org/x`, `schema:x` and expanded `[{"@value": …}]`; `compact()` turns an expanded record into the compact shape the cards read; `idIndex()` and `deref()` resolve a reference such as `audio: {"@id": "…#audio"}` to the node the record (or its `@graph`) defines.
- **Listen, Subscribe and "Their page" taps are counted**, as the NxisAI app counts them: `nxis_track_site_action` with outcome `page_open`, the action's `action_type` and `action_id`, and the site's `report_token` from the button. A tap that takes this tab to another page of the site leaves its report in the session, and the next page sends it. `reportPageOpen`, `pageOpenReport`, `setPageOpenReporter` and `MEDIA_PLAY_OUTCOME` are exported from `./tools`.
- **Pictures in the chat**. A picture record (`ImageObject`, `Photograph`) draws as a figure: the picture at its own proportions, its space kept before it loads (`width`, `height` and `aspect-ratio`, from a number, `"640"`, `"640 px"` or a QuantitativeValue), its caption and credit (`creditText`, else `copyrightHolder` or `author`), then **License** (`acquireLicensePage`; a `license` is shown, never enforced) and **Their page**. One that isn't free shows only its thumbnail. Any record's tile is its first picture (`image`, `thumbnail`, `thumbnailUrl`, `photo`, and `logo` for a place or a person), falling back to its initials. In the A2UI view a card carries its picture as the catalog's `Image` (`smallFeature`), and a lone picture draws as its figure (`emitFigureSurface`, `mediumFeature`); the Lit renderer doesn't reserve an image's space before it loads.
- `safeMediaUrl()` is the one gate every picture passes: https, or this page's own origin; never `data:`, `blob:`, `javascript:`, credentials, unfilled `{}` templates, or more than 2048 characters. Pictures load lazily, with `referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin"` and no `crossorigin`. `imagesOf`, `imageOf`, `figureOf`, `pixels` and `safeMediaUrl` are in `src/harness/playable.ts`.
- **Free episodes and videos play in the chat**, opt-in with `media="play"`. Listen, "Listen to preview" and "Listen to the latest" play in a docked **Now playing** bar between the conversation and the composer, a video in a frame above it; the bar has play/pause, a scrubber, time, speed, the transcript, the item's page and close, and keyboard controls. Only free media plays (or a preview or trailer itself marked free); paid, subscription, sign-up and expired media never does, and unmarked records only with `media-play="unmarked"`. One item at a time, through redraws and conversation switches. A Content-Security-Policy refusal, an error or a 15 s stall blocks the host for the session and turns its buttons back into links. The first play of each item reports `page_open`; every change is an `nxis-media` event. In the A2UI view, play buttons send `nxis.play` and their label reads "❚❚ Pause" while playing. Without the attribute, cards are unchanged.
- **`@nxisai/agent-harness/media`**, the player (`MediaController`, `renderDock`, `MEDIA_DOCK_CSS`), a subpath the element loads with a dynamic `import()` on the first Play tap: 5.1 KB gzip, and nothing on a page that never plays. No hls.js. `playableOf`, `mediaOf`, `mayPlay`, `playCtas`, `playLatestCtas`, `playButtonFor` and `pressMessages` are the pure parts the cards use.
- happy-dom (a dev dependency) for the player's DOM tests, opted into per file.
- The renderer spec's Watch labels (Watch, Watch the latest, Watch preview, Subscribe to watch, Sign up to watch) and an empty `media.embedHosts`, synced from its source.
- `npm run size`: size budgets for what a site pays on page load, run in CI after the build. The `ui` page-load graph (esbuild, minified, gzip) may grow at most 7 KB over the 230.5 KB measured before the media work (pictures, media detection, the access gate and the play buttons are core; the player is not); the lazy `./media` chunk stays under 10 KB gzip; hls.js and the media module never load with the page. See `scripts/size-budget.mjs`.
- `spec/renderer-spec.json`, a verbatim copy of the NxisAI renderer spec, and `npm run sync:spec -- <path>` to refresh it and regenerate `src/harness/renderer-spec.ts`. `test/renderer-spec.test.ts` fails when the two disagree.

### Fixed

- **Guardrails: no override can switch off approvals.** `overrides: { 'tool/tier': 'off' }` used to remove the confirmation for `high` and `critical` tools, and with it the bound-approval check that is documented as impossible to turn off, handoffs included. `tool/tier` can no longer be turned off: like `tool/approval`, `tool/policy`, `tool/lockdown`, `input/lockdown` and `tool/not-listed`, an override of it is ignored, and the one-time `console.warn` now says so ("can't be turned off; ignored"). A host's `tools.tier` still re-tiers a tool, but can't take one that code tiers `critical` (destructive, consequential, a payment or an account change) below `high`, so it is always confirmed; a handoff stays `critical`, and its Send card always appears. Only the value `'off'` turns a rule off.
- **Guardrails: a tool name the model invents is refused by `tool/policy`**, with a `critical` event (without the invented name in it), as the README said. The visitor still reads "I picked an unknown tool …". `Guardrails.unknown()` does it for custom engines. The README's Guardrails section now also lists the strict preset's limits for tool calls a minute (15), failed calls in a row (3), argument size (4,000 characters), generations (6) and output tokens (8,000) a turn; the `output/redact`, `tool/not-listed`, `tool/lockdown` and `input/lockdown` rules; what a rule's `confirm` does at each stage; that `debug` draws decisions in the thread; and that setting `agent.guardrails` merges shallowly and `null` resets it. The guardrails chunk grows by 88 B gzip (within its budget); the page-load graph shrinks by 24 B.
- A cited card's button, and any other card button, that opens another origin in a new tab sends no referrer (`rel="noopener noreferrer"`), as a link in a remote reply already did. `openTarget()` opens a new tab with `noopener,noreferrer`.
- The model bench runs each case that doesn't `continues` another in a browser context of its own, so a handoff a `(Send)` case left open is no longer reopened from storage by the next, unrelated case.
