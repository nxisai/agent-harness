neither implies the other — Apache-2.0 says as much in section 6.

## What this means in practice

**You may**, under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE):

- distribute your own build
- use it inside your own product

- call your fork or derivative build by a name likely to be confused with NxisAI's
- use the NxisAI name or logo in a way that suggests your build is official, endorsed, or

- publish a package to a registry under a name that implies it is ours

**You may always** say truthfully what your software does — that it is a fork of the NxisAI agent harness,
that it is compatible with NxisAI, or that it talks to the NxisAI API. Nominative use of a
trademark to describe a relationship accurately is not something we would object to, and not
something trademark law prevents.
